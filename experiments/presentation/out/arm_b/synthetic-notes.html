Field Notes on Fog

Fog is weather that has decided to stay. It doesn't arrive so much as accumulate: first the far hills go, then the near trees, then -- quite suddenly -- the end of your own street.

Three observations

  • Sound carries strangely; a gull 'over there' may be overhead.
  • Distances lie. 50 m feels like 500, and 500 like "somewhere".
  • The light is flat, so colours read about 10--15% duller than they are.

A short table

Hour Visibility Note
06:00 40 m street lamps still on
09:30 300 m hills return, one by one

Some fog-watchers keep a log with code-like entries such as vis=40m;wind=calm.1 Others just write "grey" and leave it there.
